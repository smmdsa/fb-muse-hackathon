Sub-processors and transfers

Hosting runs on Cloudflare (Workers plus D1 database) as processor under the Cloudflare customer data agreement with EU standard clauses.

No analytics, ads, fonts, or third-party SDKs load. The API is same-origin.

Your rights

Access and portability: press Download my data in the barracks. One JSON file holds every row.

Rectification: your handle is editable in the game. Other fixes go to privacy@nwart.app.

Erasure: press Delete account in the barracks. It removes the leader, sessions, challenges, agents, chips, fights, tickets, wallet rows, cooldowns, reports, and cup entries, then signs you out.

Restriction and objection: mail privacy@nwart.app. The account is paused by hand.

Requests are answered without undue delay, within one month. Identity is checked with a wallet signature. You can also complain to your data protection authority.
