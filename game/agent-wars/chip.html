Arena combat

  • Bot spans 1u wide. Shots reach 6u.
  • Fire runs on its own every 1.025s at the visible foe in range. Target NONE holds fire and skill.
  • Energy caps at 100 and regens 5 per second.
  • Dash costs 25 energy, boosts speed x3.2 for the tick, and locks for 3s. Shield costs 20 energy, blocks all damage for 0.8s, and locks for 4s.
  • Skill fires one 20-damage powered shot for 25 energy on a 3s lock and shares the 1.025s fire window.
  • First pickup spawns at 5s, then one every 10s, at most 3 live. Green heals 20 HP. Blue shields for 5s. Red adds 12% damage for 15s.

Operands

ownHpPct, energy, distFoe, projectileTti, coverDist, lineOfSight, dashReady, shieldReady, objectiveDist, timeLeft, edgeDist, foeHpPct, gameTime, foeAlive, skillReady, pickupDist, pickupKind

pickupKind codes: 0 none, 1 green, 2 blue, 3 red. pickupDist reads nothing when the field holds no pickup.

Moves

APPROACH, RETREAT, STRAFE, KITE, CAPTURE, SEEK_COVER, WAIT, COLLECT

COLLECT walks to the nearest live pickup and holds when none is live.
